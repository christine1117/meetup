Privacy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Half Sugar helps people find teammates for competitions. This page says what we keep about you, who can see it, and how to remove it.

When you sign in

You sign in with Google or LinkedIn. We get your email address and name from them. We never see your password.

Your profile

  • Visible to people signed in: nickname, field, city, how you like to meet, what you are curious about, and your fun fact.
  • Sealed: real name, job title, school, company and contact. Only you can see them, plus the people you team up with, after both of you agree.

Kept only in your browser

Your competition cards, teams, chats and language choice stay in this browser for now. They are not sent to us.

Where it is stored

Accounts and profiles are stored with Supabase, our database provider. The site is served by Cloudflare. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

Competitions

Competition details come from public pages on Devpost, MLH and 獎金獵人, and link back to them.

Changing or removing your data

  • Edit your profile any time on the My order page.
  • Log out removes your profile from this browser.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, write to the support email shown on Google’s sign-in screen for Half Sugar.

隱私權說明

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 31 日

Half Sugar 幫大家找比賽隊友。這一頁說明我們會留下你的哪些資料、誰看得到，以及怎麼刪掉。

登入時

你用 Google 或 LinkedIn 登入。我們只會拿到你的 email 和名字，看不到你的密碼。

個人資料

  • 登入的人都看得到：暱稱、領域、城市、喜歡怎麼見面、好奇的事、冷知識。
  • 封起來的：本名、職稱、學校、公司、聯絡方式。只有你自己看得到，還有在雙方都同意組隊後，你的隊友看得到。

只存在你的瀏覽器

比賽卡、隊伍、聊天和語言設定，目前只存在這個瀏覽器裡，不會傳給我們。

存在哪裡

帳號和個人資料存在 Supabase（我們用的資料庫服務）。網站由 Cloudflare 提供。我們不賣資料、不放廣告，也不用追蹤 cookie。

比賽資訊

比賽資訊來自 Devpost、MLH 和獎金獵人的公開頁面，並附上原始連結。

修改或刪除資料

  • 可隨時在「我的訂單」頁面修改個人資料。
  • 登出會將個人資料從此瀏覽器移除。
  • 想刪除整個帳號，請寄信到 Google 登入畫面上 Half Sugar 顯示的支援 email。
